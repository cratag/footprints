Upload your Google Maps Timeline export and replay each day step by step on the map. Everything is processed in your browser.

How do I export my routes?

Google stores your Timeline on your phone, so recent routes are exported from there.

📱 Android · iPhone

  1. Open Google Maps → your profile picture → Your Timeline.
  2. Tap ⋮ (or ⋯ on iPhone) → Location and privacy settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom, tap Export Timeline data and save the file.
